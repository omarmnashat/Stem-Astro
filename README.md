# 🚀 ExoLife — Explore. Survive. Build Beyond Earth.

**ExoLife** is an interactive 3D space habitat simulation and mission-training platform designed to introduce users to the challenges of human exploration and long-term survival on the Moon and Mars.

Built with **React, Three.js, and modern web technologies**, ExoLife transforms space exploration into an immersive, hands-on experience where players can explore extraterrestrial environments, operate mission systems, and collaborate with other crew members in a simulated space mission.

## 🌌 Explore. Build. Survive.

ExoLife puts players in the role of space explorers responsible for establishing and maintaining sustainable human habitats beyond Earth. Through interactive mission scenarios and resource-management systems, players can:

- **Explore Lunar and Martian Environments:** Navigate 3D extraterrestrial landscapes and discover scientifically inspired locations.
- **Execute Space Missions:** Experience spacecraft landing scenarios and mission operations.
- **Perform Scientific Exploration:** Excavate regolith layers, investigate mineral outcrops, and collect samples for scientific analysis.
- **Build Sustainable Habitats:** Construct modular outposts featuring solar arrays, oxygen-producing Veggie Bio-Domes, and other essential infrastructure.
- **Manage Mission Resources:** Monitor oxygen, water, electricity, food supplies, radiation exposure, and crew health.
- **Collaborate as a Crew:** Experience cooperative mission activities and shared objectives across multiple devices.

## 🛰️ The Science Behind ExoLife

ExoLife is inspired by real-world aerospace engineering, astrobiology, and **In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)** concepts.

The simulation introduces users to the complexity of maintaining a sustainable space habitat, where every decision can affect mission efficiency and crew survival.

Players must balance interconnected life-support systems, manage limited resources, and respond to unexpected mission challenges while learning about the technologies and scientific principles that could support future human exploration.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **React** — Frontend application and interactive user interface.
- **Three.js** — Real-time 3D environments and visualization.
- **Vite** — Development and build tooling.
- **Tailwind CSS** — Responsive interface styling.
- **React Router** — Application navigation.
- **NASA Open APIs & Space Science Resources** — Scientific data and inspiration for selected space-related features.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make complex space science and engineering concepts accessible through interactive learning.

By combining 3D simulation, resource management, scientific exploration, and mission-based challenges, ExoLife aims to bridge the gap between theoretical STEM education and practical space exploration concepts.

We hope to inspire the next generation of scientists, engineers, programmers, and space explorers to take part in humanity's journey toward a sustainable future beyond Earth.
